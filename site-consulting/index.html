Member value case

Frequent visitors report higher baskets; parking must demonstrate that it causes additional valuable visits.

Observed association

Commercial interpretation

  • Prioritize members whose repeat visits are valuable to the mall.
  • Separate tenant turnover from the landlord’s effective share.
  • Measure incremental trips against a comparable group or baseline.
  • Avoid converting the 68% basket difference directly into a parking-driven uplift.
Value bridge: additional visits × spend per visit × effective mall capture, less additional service and support costs.
Sources: S4: Vase.ai, Malaysia Shopping Mall Report 2026 (n=500). Survey association is not causal and is not a KL East estimate. Effective capture is a finance-calibrated scenario.
