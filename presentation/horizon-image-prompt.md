## Final prompt

Use case: productivity-visual. Asset type: wide illustration for a professional business presentation, white background. Create an elegant three-horizon strategy diagram with three staggered ascending S-curves, flowing left to right like the classic three horizons growth model. First curve blue at lower left, second muted gold rising in the middle, third teal rising at upper right. Each starts as a thin smooth line, accelerates, then levels into a plateau; subtle pale colored area beneath each segment. Minimal thin charcoal horizontal axis with right arrow, no vertical axis, no data ticks, no grid. Clean flat editorial vector-like finish, generous negative space. Landscape 3:1 composition. No text, no numbers, no legends, no icons, no decorative objects, no shadows or 3D. This is a qualitative strategic illustration, not a quantitative financial chart. Keep all curves within the central 85 percent so editable HTML labels can sit above and below.
