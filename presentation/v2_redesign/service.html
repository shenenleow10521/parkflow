parkflowIntroducing ParkFlow

A reservation-based parking service for a dedicated Priority Zone.

Let shoppers reserve access to a ~100-bay Priority Zone near the mall lifts, with capacity planned between reservations and walk-ins.

How ParkFlow works

  1. 01
    Reserve access

    Book a space in the Priority Zone before arrival, via the ParkFlow app.

  2. 02
    Verify at entry

    Zone LPR cameras recognise the plate, confirm the booking and admit the car.

  3. 03
    Park in any available bay

    Choose any free bay in the shared zone, close to the mall lifts.

Intelligent capacity management

Demand forecasts and a rolling three-hour plan set how many spaces open for reservations. Current free capacity and accepted bookings decide every admission, so walk-ins keep their space.

For shoppers

A more certain, time-saving parking experience.

For SDP

Utilisation, member value and booking income to test in the pilot.

For the portfolio

Prove the model at one site, then replicate across SDP malls.

Proposed service. ~100 bays matches the reference zone used in the offline simulations; zone size, layout, LPR placement and live deployment remain to be confirmed in the site audit. Benefits are pilot hypotheses, not measured results.
