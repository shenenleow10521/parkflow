Reserve capacity,not parking bays.

ParkFlow gives mall members priority parking without locking a single bay. Members reserve a share of a zone's capacity, every uncommitted space stays open to all shoppers, and the entrance gate decides who comes in, live.

Why should parking privileges cost parking efficiency?

Malls want to give members something they feel on every visit. Today that usually means taking bays away from everyone else.

Membership feels thin

Members collect shopping rewards, but parking, the first thing they do at the mall, gives them nothing reliable.

VIP bays sit empty

Permanent member-only bays stay unused off-peak, while ordinary shoppers circle the floor below looking for a space.

Hardware on every bay

Bay locks and per-bay sensors add installation and maintenance cost to every single space a mall wants to reserve.

The opportunity

Give members priority parking without permanently removing capacity from general shoppers.

Members never get a specific bay, only a guaranteed share of the zone. Bookings are confirmed only when that share can be protected; forecasts help planning but never replace this check at the gate.

The reservation engine, in five steps.

Built from parts most malls already own: CCTV, an entry gate and an app.

Important: ParkFlow only confirms a reservation when it can protect the capacity. AI forecasts inform planning; they never replace the real-time admission check.

  1. 1

    Detect occupancy

    Existing CCTV feeds estimate occupied spaces with computer vision. Number-plate (ANPR) entry and exit records give a second, independent count. No sensor in each bay.

  2. 2

    Set member-priority capacity

    The operator sets the most priority reservations a zone may hold at the same time. For example, a 100-space zone supports up to 20 overlapping member reservations.

  3. 3

    Accept reservations

    Members give their arrival time and expected stay. Before confirming, ParkFlow checks:

    • membership and the registered number plate
    • overlapping reservations
    • physical capacity available
    • the protected quota and safety margin
  4. 4

    Control shared-zone access

    Every arriving car meets the gate rule. While public capacity remains, the sign is green and everyone enters. At zero it turns amber: other shoppers are sent to another zone and confirmed members enter after the plate check.

    100 − 75 − 20 − 5 = 0 open to new non-member cars
  5. 5

    Release and reallocate

    Each reservation has a grace period the operator can set. No-shows release their protected capacity automatically, departures update occupancy, and overstays or detection errors raise an alert and can pause new bookings.

One system, three beneficiaries.

Primary customer

Mall operators

  • Stronger membership programmes and loyalty
  • Better use of the car park they already have
  • New revenue without major infrastructure spending
  • Live occupancy, reservations and demand forecasts in one place
Primary end users

Mall members

  • Reserve 30 to 60 minutes before arriving
  • Say how long they plan to stay
  • Enter through number-plate check, no ticket
  • Park in any free bay inside the reserved zone
Secondary users

General shoppers

  • See availability by floor and zone before arriving: general, Ladies' parking and member-priority
  • Still use the member-priority zone whenever uncommitted space remains

Proven parts, new logic.

The innovation is in how established technologies are combined, and in the capacity rule that connects them.

Technology

CCTV vehicle detectionANPR plate recognitionRule-based reservationsWeb and mobile appsHistorical-data forecasting

What a site needs

  • CCTV coverage good enough to estimate occupancy
  • ANPR cameras at the zone entrance and exit
  • A compatible boom gate and access controller
  • Cloud or on-site processing
  • The driver app and the operator dashboard

What we will test openly

  • Camera blind spots and occupancy accuracy
  • Access to existing infrastructure and footage
  • Vehicle data privacy and security
  • Overstays, booking conflicts and gate exceptions
