L2Our idea

Promise the Priority Zone to members. Guide everyone else through the wider car park.

Example: one evening, 12 spots next to the lifts.

L1The solution

One gated Priority Zone. One capacity truth.

The Priority Zone is a members-only service beside the B1 lifts. Its bookable capacity comes from verified entry, verified exit and active reservations—not from a forecast.

Book only what exists

The booking engine accepts a member only when the deterministic ledger shows capacity.

Verify in and out

Automatic number-plate recognition opens the gate for an eligible booking and closes the loop on exit.

Forecast the whole car park

Members see likely availability and departure windows across the Priority Zone and general levels before choosing when to book.

Eligibility, booking window and no-show policy are pilot design choices. The commercial case does not assume public booking fees.

GControl versus forecast

The gate guarantees. The forecast advises.

These are intentionally separate. Entry, exit and reservations determine whether another booking is allowed. Forecasting only helps a member decide when to travel or try again.

The forecast never creates capacity. Only a verified exit or a released reservation can make another booking available.

B1The prototype

Members decide before they join the queue.

  1. Check the whole car park

    See live open-space data and likely full periods across every level.

  2. Book the Priority Zone

    If deterministic capacity is available, reserve member access for the chosen window.

  3. Enter by registered plate

    The gate verifies the booking; no QR code or manual check is required.

  4. Leave and release capacity

    The exit verifier closes the visit and returns the bay to the booking ledger.

B2The proof

We tested it on 1,000 busy Saturdays, in a computer simulation.

We can't wait for 1,000 real Saturdays, so we built them in a computer and tried every option on exactly the same days.

  1. Create a busy day

    Crowds at lunch and after 5 pm, most shoppers staying about 1.5 hours, 60 bookings, and some people who never show up.

  2. Try every option on it

    No booking at all, a fixed VIP area, a simple counting rule, and ParkFlow, on the same 100 spots.

  3. Repeat 1,000 times

    Then check: did everyone who booked get a spot? How long did spots sit empty while other drivers were turned away?
