Priority Zone Access Rules

Member eligibility

Both modes remain members-only. A walk-in is an eligible member without a booking.

Admission control

Before a walk-in enters, verified empty capacity must cover outstanding arriving bookings, the safety buffer and that new admission.

Mode switching

Forecast demand guides the switch to Priority mode or a return to Normal mode. A predicted departure cannot create bookable capacity.

Existing parked members

Priority mode restricts new walk-in entry. Members already inside stay until they leave.

Pilot validation

Agree buffer size, switching thresholds and operating responsibilities before launch. Local forecasts and the switching policy require field validation.
