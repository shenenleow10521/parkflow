KLGCC Mall LG car park, with ParkFlow

Our 9 Nov 2026 walkthrough of the LG level, rebuilt as a 3D digital twin. Before and after, how a Priority Zone booking is accepted, the machine learning behind it, the member booking flow and the admin dashboard.

What is real

The footage is our own 5-minute phone walkthrough of LG. The pillar grid (D to U, rows 2 to 7), OKU bays, motorcycle area and exits come from that footage.

What is approximate

Bay count and dimensions are estimated from the video, not from official drawings. Bookings, occupancy and dashboard figures are simulated.

Model figures

99.05% bay detection and 1.41% forecast error come from our tests on public datasets (CNRPark+EXT, 30 car parks). A KLGCC pilot still needs labelled LG frames.
