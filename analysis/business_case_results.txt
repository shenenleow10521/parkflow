SDP ON ITS OWN (4 malls, no partners), base case, per year
  Revenue RM84,199; running incl. engineering RM90,000; one-off RM232,560 spread over 5 years RM46,512
  Net cost RM52,313 a year = RM13,078 per mall, or RM1.80 per booking served (29,014 a year)
  With a card sponsor and Priority Pass (pilot targets): net RM+139,879 a year
  Fees alone would need about 70 paid bookings per busy day per mall (base 30), so for SDP it is a loyalty and footfall programme, not a fee business
  A partner mall (1.5 zones) pays RM73,692 a year (licence, running, hardware over 5 years) against RM31,575 of fees: it buys ParkFlow for footfall and loyalty, as SDP does; so partner licences are upside, not the base case.

Not counted (upside for SDP): rewards sign-ups and voucher-led visits, spend data, turnover rent from higher tenant sales, search time saved (simulate_carpark.py). The premium rate from non-bookers goes to the parking operator (A8).
