SDP ON ITS OWN (4 malls, no partners), base case, per year
  Revenue RM84,199; running incl. engineer RM162,000; one-off RM322,560 spread over 5 years RM64,512
  Net cost RM142,313 a year = RM9 per SDP home near the 4 malls (15,600 homes), or RM4.9 per booking served (29,014 a year)
  Fees alone would need about 140 paid bookings per busy day per mall (base 30), so for SDP it is a loyalty and footfall programme, not a fee business
  A partner mall pays RM49,128 a year (licence, running, hardware over 5 years) against RM21,050 of fees: it buys ParkFlow for footfall and loyalty, as SDP does; so partner licences are upside, not the base case.

Not counted (upside for SDP): PRIME value for home sales, spend data, turnover rent from higher tenant sales, premium-rate income from non-bookers, search time saved (simulate_carpark.py).
