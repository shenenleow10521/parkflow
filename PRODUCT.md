# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary audience is Sime Darby Property hackathon judges and business decision-makers across mall management, parking operations, loyalty and digital, IT and privacy, and finance. They need to understand the operating model quickly, challenge its assumptions, and decide whether KL East Mall should host a measured pilot.

## Product Purpose

ParkFlow is a members-first parking proposition for KL East Mall. A gated Priority Zone uses entry and exit license-plate verification plus deterministic available-to-promise control to protect accepted bookings. Whole-car-park forecasting helps members decide when to book and helps all drivers find general parking, while giving the operator forward-looking occupancy and flow data. The immediate goal is approval for a bounded KL East lighthouse pilot, not a national rollout or an assumed standalone startup.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, mechanical, credible. The experience should feel like walking through a live car park control system, then entering a disciplined investment-committee review. The voice is specific, evidence-led, calm under scrutiny, and honest about what the pilot still needs to prove.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages with feature-card grids, vague claims, inflated ROI, or decorative glass effects.
- Startup pitch pages that lead with TAM, aggressive licensing projections, or public booking fees before proving the KL East use case.
- Consulting pages that reduce the product to static tables and lose the physical parking experience.
- Dense technical explainers that blur deterministic booking control with probabilistic forecasting.
- Animations that delay reading, obscure content, or make the business section feel promotional.

## Design Principles

1. Make the guarantee legible: deterministic booking control and forecasting are separate layers.
2. Show the physical system: use the Priority Zone, gate states, member app, and operator screen as evidence.
3. Answer first, then substantiate: each business section leads with a decision or implication.
4. Separate evidence from hypotheses: verified benchmarks, planning assumptions, and pilot questions are visibly distinct.
5. Earn scale through proof: KL East lighthouse, measured value gate, qualified SDP portfolio, then an external platform option.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard usability. Preserve visible focus states, semantic headings, meaningful alternative text, and information that does not rely on colour alone. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by removing non-essential movement while keeping all content visible.
